3Pharmacily — the application

Every screen the pharmacy needs, built on the Pharmacily language: mint ground, white cards at 20px, one deep green for action, and the four pastels holding their subjects — green for sales, teal for stock, rose for expiry, lilac for people. Desktop is 1280×800, mobile 390×844. Drug images are live slots you can drop photos onto.

Pharmacily
Design language · v1

A calm, pastel counter for people who work fast.

Pharmacily runs on a pale mint ground with soft white cards, one deep green for action, and a four-colour pastel set that carries meaning — sales, stock, expiry, people. Corners are generous, shadows are barely there, and colour is a tint behind information rather than a fill over it.

Pale mint ground 20px card radius Outfit + Plus Jakarta Sans Phosphor icons, fill for active

01 · Logo sketches

Five directions — vector sketches, not finished artwork
Capsule cross

Two capsules crossing make the care mark. Reads at 16px, works as a favicon, and the two pastels carry the palette.

Leaf in the ring

Herbal and human rather than clinical. The ring gives it a stamp quality for receipts and door glass.

P monogram tile

The quietest option and the most app-like. Tile colour can shift per branch without touching the letter.

Mortar and pestle

The most literally pharmacy of the set. Warmest for signage; needs the most care at small sizes.

Dose arc

Three doses under a shelter arc. The one that shows all four palette colours, so it sets the tone fastest.

On the dark ground

Reverse the mark rather than recolouring the wordmark. Minimum mark size 20px; clear space equals half the tile.

Each pastel owns one subject and never drifts: green is money in, teal is what's on the shelf, rose is time running out, lilac is people and accounts. Tinted cards carry their own dark ink from the same hue — never grey text on a pastel, and never a pastel as body copy.

Rules
  • Never bolder than 600 — hierarchy is size and space.
  • Money, counts and dates use tabular numerals.
  • Body copy 15px minimum; table rows 13.5px minimum.
  • Sentence case everywhere except the .1em uppercase label.
Voice

Plain and specific. "5 items expire within 30 days" beats "Attention required". Name the drug, the number and the date; skip the exclamation mark.

04 · Shape, space and elevation

Soft corners, an 8px rhythm, almost no shadow
Radius
10 inputs and thumbnails · 14 nav items and tiles · 20 cards and panels · full round for pills, chips and avatars.
Space
4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48. Card padding 24, gap between cards 20, page gutter 40.
Elevation
Resting cards, floating menus, modals. Green-tinted shadow, never grey or black. No borders on white cards.

06 · Data, icons and photography

How numbers and pack shots behave
Charts
Bars are fully rounded and always sit in a pale mint track, so an empty day still has a shape. Today is the one deep-green bar. Series colours come from the pastel set, ordered green, teal, rose, lilac, then peach and orchid for a sixth and seventh.
Icons
Phosphor regular at 16–20px, fill weight only for the active nav item. Icons live in soft circles, never bare next to text.
Pack photography
Pack
photo
Square, 10px radius, shot on white and cropped tight to the box. When there's no photo the fallback is a pastel tile with the category icon — tinted by the drug's status, so a list never looks broken.
A note on fit

This language and the Industry system want different things.

Your reference is soft, pastel and fully rounded; Industry is a square steel wireframe on paper. I followed the reference, since that's the look you handed me — so Pharmacily is its own system, not a variant of Industry. Tell me which one wins and I'll rebuild the twenty-two screens on it.
